OUR STORY

From a chance meeting
to forever.

We met once,
then life carried us in different directions.

For a while, we continued on separate paths,
never knowing that our story was not yet over.

Until one unexpected call
brought us back to each other.

From that moment,
we began to rediscover the connection
we had once shared.

For ten months,
we loved across the distance—
through miles, silence, and longing.

We learned to cherish every conversation,
every message, every call,
and every moment we were able to share.

There were days when the distance felt difficult,
when we wished we could simply be together,
but every mile only made our hearts
draw closer.

Through patience, trust, and understanding,
we continued to choose one another,
even when being apart was not easy.

What began as a chance meeting
became a second chance.

And that second chance
slowly grew into a love
we could no longer imagine living without.

Over time, we realized
that love is not only found in the moments we share,
but also in the commitment to stay,
to understand, and to keep choosing each other.

Now, after every distance, every waiting,
and every moment that brought us closer,
we are ready to begin the next chapter together.

Today, we choose each other—
with grateful hearts,
with endless love,
and with the promise to walk side by side
through all that life may bring.

Today, tomorrow,
and for all the days to come.
